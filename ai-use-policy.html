AI Use Policy

Effective January 1, 2026

Our Position

At True Haven Press, we believe the creative work must be human. The premise, the characters, the plot or argument, and the voice on the page are the art. Using tools to refine that work into a commercially viable book has always been part of publishing: dictionaries, style manuals, editors, proofreaders, and grammar and consistency checkers. AI tools are the newest additions to that toolbox, and we hold them to the same rule:

A tool may help you refine and polish what you created. It may not create it for you.

This policy explains what that means for authors we publish, what we require be disclosed, and how our own team uses and does not use AI.

How Authors May Use AI

Accepted. These uses are AI-assisted and do not affect whether we consider your manuscript:

  • Checking spelling, grammar, punctuation, and consistency in text you wrote
  • Suggesting ways to improve the clarity, flow, or concision of text you wrote, where you decide what to keep
  • Brainstorming, research, and outlining, where you then write the text yourself
  • Copyediting help before professional editing
  • Using an AI tool to organize material you supplied, such as your plot, characters, and story notes, or your emails, SOPs, and business documents, into a draft that suggests structure and content, provided you then re-write in your own words, so that no AI-written sentences remain in the manuscript

Disclose and discuss. These uses don’t rule a manuscript out, but we need to talk them through before we make an offer because they change how the book must be listed and credited:

  • Passages that began as AI-written sentences and were revised rather than rewritten, even if heavily revised
  • AI-generated images, illustrations, or cover art, even if you edited them afterward
  • AI-generated translations

Not accepted. We will not publish:

  • Works in which AI originated the story, characters, or central ideas, or produced most of the text
  • Works generated by AI and only lightly edited
  • Content that reproduces or imitates another author’s protected work

Disclosure

Tell us how you used AI by completing the questionnaire we’ll provide. This then becomes your statement of disclosure on file.

We ask because the answer affects how your book is listed and protected, not to police your creative process. Three sets of rules beyond our control are present in book publishing:

  • Amazon KDP. Amazon requires authors to disclose AI-generated text, images, and translations at publication, including AI-generated text the author edited afterward. AI-assisted use, such as editing and refining your own writing, does not require disclosure.
  • IngramSpark. IngramSpark accepts work in which generative AI was used only in an assistive way. Where generative AI contributed content, it requires significant human editorial contribution and proper attribution.
  • Copyright registration. The US Copyright Office requires AI-generated material beyond a minimal amount to be disclosed and excluded from a copyright registration. AI-generated material itself cannot be copyrighted; only the human-authored parts of a work are protected.

These rules change, so we check them each time at publication and answer each platform’s questions based on what you have told us.

If you’re unsure which side of that line a passage falls on, tell us. We’d rather sort it out with you before publication than have a platform sort it out after and refuse to publish a product you’ve invested in producing.

If your process changes during the project, such as during revisions, let us know in writing.

If AI Use Is Not Disclosed

Accurate disclosure protects your listing and our imprint. If we learn that AI use was materially misrepresented, we may withhold the True Haven Press imprint from the book, decline to publish it, or end the engagement under the terms of your agreement. As with any parting of ways, the completed work and assets you have paid for remain yours.

How We Use AI

Your book is edited by trained, experienced humans. No generative AI writes, rewrites, or edits your manuscript at any stage.

Here is precisely who uses what:

  • Editors. No AI. Developmental, substantive and structural, line, and copy editing are human work from start to finish.
  • Proofreaders. A full human read-through, plus PerfectIt as a quality-assurance pass. PerfectIt is a consistency checker that flags inconsistent hyphenation, capitalization, abbreviation, and departures from your style sheet. It does not generate or rewrite text, and the proofreader reviews every flagged instance and decides what to change.
  • Managing editors and marketing specialists. We use Claude for researching and developing categories, keywords, and marketing content. A human expert reviews, verifies, and finalizes every result before it is used anywhere.
  • Ghostwriting business lead magnets. We use Claude to organize all of the materials a business owner provides and suggest a manuscript outline. The outline is then used to conduct market research, again using Claude, to see how the writer can best make the book stand out in a crowd and achieve the business owner’s objectives. Finally, the ghostwriter drafts a complete manuscript, then uses Claude to analyze for repetition and adherence to the business owner’s voice. Those results inform edits the ghostwriter makes to the draft before presenting it to the client for review and revisions.

Your Manuscript Stays Yours

Every AI tool we use runs on company-held paid accounts configured so that nothing uploaded or referenced can be used for AI training or shared beyond that account. Your manuscript is not training data, and it does not leave our control.
